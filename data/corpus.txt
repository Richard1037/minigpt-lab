the small town sits between two hills and a slow river .
in the morning the river is grey and the hills are blue .
people walk to the market before the sun comes over the hill .
the market has bread and apples and old books on a table .
a dog sleeps in the sun near the door of the shop .
the shop keeper reads a book and does not look up .
children run to the school with loud voices and clean shoes .
the teacher writes one word on the black board and asks what it means .
no one knows the word , so the teacher smiles and writes another .
in the afternoon the rain comes down from the west hill .
the river turns brown and the small boats shake at the rope .
an old man walks home with no hat and no umbrella in the rain .
he says the rain is good for the fields and bad for the bread .
the fields are green in spring and gold in the warm summer .
farmers take the grain to town and sell it by the hour .
at night the town is quiet but not dark , the stars are close .
the river keeps moving and no one hears it move .
a bird sings once and then stops to listen to the water .
the light in the window of the library stays on until late .
a girl reads there and writes small notes on a yellow page .
she wants to learn ten new words a day and keep them for a year .
the words are hard but the book is kind and the room is warm .
her brother waits outside with two apples and a cold wind .
they walk home under one lamp and count the steps to the door .
their mother asks what they learned and they answer all at once .
the father says nothing about the rain but he mends the old roof .
in the morning the sun comes back over the blue hill again .
the river is grey and the market is full and the dog is warm .
the school bell rings and the children say the new words out loud .
the teacher writes the old word again and this time every hand goes up .
the town sits between two hills and a slow moving river .
nothing new happens here , and that is the news of the day .
the bread is sold , the books are read , the boats are safe .
a bird sings , a girl writes , the lamp stays on until late .
the rain of the night is dry by noon and good for the field .
the small town keeps its own time and does not ask for ours .
by the road a man sells milk and says it is still warm .
two boys buy one cup and share it on the stone by the gate .
the gate is old and the stone is smooth from many years of rain .
in the library a lamp burns low and the girl turns one more page .
she counts the words she knows and the number makes her laugh .
outside the wind takes her note and puts it in the river .
the river keeps the note and moves on to the fields and the town .
the farmer finds it in the morning and cannot read the small hand .
he takes it to the teacher who reads it out loud to the class .
the children say it is a good note and ask for a lesson on water .
the school has no boat , so they draw one on the black board .
after school the girl walks home and tells her brother all about it .
their father has mended the roof and the house is dry and warm .
supper is bread and milk and the last apples of the year .
the mother reads the note again and keeps it in her old book .
night comes and the hills go dark before the town lights do .
a dog moves from the door to the fire and sleeps on the warm stone .
the river is loud in the dark and quiet in the light of the day .
no ship comes here , only small boats and one old grey bridge .
in the spring the two hills are green and the water runs high .
in the summer the market has melons and the children have long days .
in the autumn the grain is gold and the town smells of bread and rain .
in the winter the river is still and the lamps stay on all night .
the small town sits between two hills and waits for the same road .
the road brings one letter a week and the letter says the same thing .
the fields are well , the boats are safe , the school keeps its word .
a bird sings in the morning and stops to listen to the slow water .
the girl writes one more note and keeps it in her pocket this time .
